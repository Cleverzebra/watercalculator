The Restaurant Guide.

One big eating list for locals — hidden gems, long-running institutions, and neighborhood favorites, mostly $ to $$, spread across the city and suburbs. Every spot below carries a recent (2025–2026) signal of operation. The tourist-priced stuff is deliberately left out.

The weekend rule: the brunch spots and clam shacks here get punishing lines Sat/Sun 10am–1pm and on the first warm weekends of summer. Go on a weekday or right at open and you skip almost all of it. Cash-only and no-reservations spots are flagged inline.
