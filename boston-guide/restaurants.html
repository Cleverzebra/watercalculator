Eat: the all-vegetarian edition

Your house speaks fluent vegetarian, so this list does too. Half of it is fully meat-free; the other half is omnivore rooms where a vegetarian orders like a regular, not a special request. Every single spot carries a July-2026 open signal — a rule that matters more than usual this year, because Boston’s veg scene just lived through a plot twist (see the Clover saga below).

🥕 Fully Veg & Vegan 👑 Where Veg Eats Like Royalty 🕯️ Date-Night Tables 🧋 Teen-Cheap Eats 🍦 Sweets & Ice Cream 💰 The $12 Hall of Fame
The Clover saga, because it explains the whole list: Boston’s beloved vegetarian fast-casual chain closed every location on May 28, 2026 — and reopened six of them on June 9 after an investor rescue and a genuine public outcry. Moral: the veg scene is alive but volatile, so this guide only lists places with a fresh 2026 pulse, and flags the recently fallen at the bottom so you don’t drive to a ghost.

The home turf

🥕 Fully vegetarian & vegan

Places where the whole menu is yours. The ⭐ marks the house favorites worth crossing town for.

📍 Central Sq, Cambridge · Red Line

⭐ Veggie Galaxy — the landmark

A retro chrome diner where everything is vegetarian and everything can be made vegan — all-day breakfast, seitan clubs, vegan frappes, and a 100%-vegan bakery. Just crowned America’s best vegan-friendly diner. No reservations; counter seats move fastest.

$–$$💕 Kitschy-romanticRed ✓

veggiegalaxy.com

📍 Inman · Central · Kendall · FiDi · Pru · Harvard Science Ctr

⭐ Clover Food Lab — the comeback

The chickpea-fritter pita and rosemary fries are still the best $10 vegetarian lunch in town, now with a resurrection narrative. Six locations survived the June 2026 rescue (Harvard Square proper is still dark). Meatless by design, mostly vegan in practice.

$Lunch heroRed ✓

cloverfoodlab.com

📍 Chinatown (3 Beach St, 2nd floor) · Orange Line

⭐ My Thai Vegan Cafe — the hidden teahouse

A 100% vegan Thai teahouse up a Chinatown staircase, 23 years running: mock-duck noodle soup, dumplings, mango fried rice, and proper boba. One of the best cheap-date rooms in the city — the kids should claim it.

$💕 DateOrange ✓

mythaivegancafe.com

📍 Allston (1 Brighton Ave) · Green B / 66 bus

⭐ Grasshopper — the vegan Chinese OG

25+ years of vegan Chinese-Vietnamese: the No-Name (fried gluten + veggies), kale-gluten in black bean sauce, huge portions, zero pretense — plus a monthly all-you-can-eat buffet (third Sundays). The answer to “can we get Chinese?”

$Green B ✓

grasshopperveganboston.com

📍 South Boston + Kendall Sq (new, June 2026)

⭐ Lulu Green — the current star

Boston Magazine’s Best Vegan Restaurant two years running: Middle-East-inflected café-by-day, bar-by-night. The new Kendall location has a 22-seat covered garden patio with a pond — instantly one of the best veg date venues in the city. Hot-honey crispy chick’n sandwich, tacos al pastor, pistachio rose latte.

$$💕 Patio dateRed ✓ (Kendall)

lulugreen.com

📍 Union Sq, Somerville · GLX · Thu–Sun only

⭐ Koshari Mama — Egyptian street food

Mother-daughter vegan Egyptian: the namesake koshari (lentils, rice, pasta, crispy onions — carb-on-carb perfection, ~$11), mushroom shawarma, om ali for dessert. Cult favorite, deservedly. Note the Thursday–Sunday schedule.

$GLX ✓

kosharimama.com

📍 10+ locations (Central, Harvard, Back Bay, Salem, Brookline…)

Life Alive — the weekday default

Warm grain bowls, wraps and smoothies; heavily vegan. The Goddess bowl is the signature. A Seaport location with a harbor patio is opening this month. It’s the “we need dinner and nobody wants to think” solution.

$–$$Weeknight ✓Red ✓

lifealive.com

📍 Watertown (600 Mt Auburn St) · bus 71

Red Lentil — the west-side standby

Chef Pankaj Pradhan’s globally-spiced vegetarian/vegan kitchen — sweet-potato-quinoa cakes, seitan piccata — and the best veg weekend brunch on your side of the river.

$$Weekend brunch

theredlentil.com

📍 Brookline Village + Union Sq (+ Norwood, Lowell)

Veggie Crust — Indian-fusion pizza

Falafel pizza, paneer-tikka pizza, Impossible toppings, vegan cheese on anything; the Brookline shop is kosher. Shareable, teen-proof, and genuinely its own thing.

$–$$Green D / GLX ✓

vegcrust.com

📍 Union Sq, Somerville · GLX

Dosa n Curry — the pure-veg Indian

South Indian + Indo-Chinese, all vegetarian with big vegan and Jain menus: plate-overhanging masala dosas (~$10–13), vegan nice-cream after. Fluorescent-lit and proud of it.

$GLX ✓

dosa-n-curry.com

📍 Allston (1096 Comm Ave) · Green B

Rhythm ’n Wraps

Black-owned vegan wraps and burgers spanning Caribbean–Indian–Mexican; the award-winning chick’n sandwich and the Big Shug Impossible burger are the orders.

$Green B ✓

rhythmnwraps.com

📍 Jamaica Plain (61 South St) + Hub Hall stall

Juicy Greens

Vegan café/juice bar — plant-based bowls, arepas, and toasts. Daytime fuel before an Arboretum walk with the dog.

Omnivore rooms, vegetarian thrones

👑 Where vegetarians eat like royalty

Cuisines built around vegetables — meze, thali, injera platters, falafel, dosa — where the veg order is the point, not the fallback.

The Sortun axis (your special-occasion default)

📍 Inman Sq, Cambridge

⭐ Oleana — the veg date, definitively

Ana Sortun’s Turkish/Eastern-Med icon offers a dedicated vegetarian tasting, and the meze format means half the menu is vegetable-driven anyway (spinach falafel, whipped feta with hot pepper). In July, the fig-tree fountain patio is the most coveted veg-friendly seat in Boston — it’s first-come, so arrive at opening.

$$–$$$💕 The onePatio in summer

oleanarestaurant.com

📍 Cambridge/Watertown line (1 Belmont St) · bus 71/73

⭐ Sofra — the daytime version

Sortun’s bakery-café: mezze plates, halloumi, shakshuka, tahini brioche, legendary morning buns. Open 8am–5pm — a weekday-morning treat-yourself move, and the best picnic-provisioner for Crane Beach runs.

$Weekday AM💕 Daytime date

sofrabakery.com

Veg pho: Pho Pasteur & Le’s

Vegetable/tofu pho with the works — Pho Pasteur (Chinatown, 10am–9:30pm daily) and Le’s (Harvard Sq; the Allston shop closed). For marked-vegetarian dim sum, Winsor Dim Sum Cafe (10 Tyler St) makes veg dumplings to order.

Anna’s Taqueria & Villa Mexico

Tenoch’s rajas quesadillas and El Pelon Brighton (Fenway original closed) round it out.

🍦 Sweets & ice cream (vegan-strong)

📍 Fenway (140 Brookline Ave) — last one standing

⭐ FoMu

The all-vegan coconut-base ice cream maker is down to its Fenway scoop shop (open late) — avocado-lime and maple walnut remain elite. Pints live on in groceries and farmers markets.

$Green D ✓

Toscanini’s

B3 (brown butter, brown sugar, brownie) is the city’s most famous flavor; sorbets and occasional vegan flavors — ask. Tosci’s-to-Graffiti-Alley is a complete date.
