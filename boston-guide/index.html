Zero-decision mode

🗂️ Ready-made playbooks

For the days when nobody wants to plan. Each one is a real sequence you can run as written — timings, links, and an honest cost.

You · any weekday

The 2-hour evening reset

  1. Skip the errand. Hit a weekday evening farmers market for dinner ingredients.
  2. Golden hour at the Kendall roof garden or Post Office Square.
  3. Optional add-on: a Life Alive bowl on the walk back.
Free–$15

You · Sunday, early

The flea-market circuit

  1. Todd Farm by 7:30am — the good stuff goes first.
  2. Loop through Rockport or Essex on the way home.
  3. Veg lunch is flagged in every town writeup — no scrambling.
$ gas + whatever you find

The kids · Thursday

The $25 all-in date

  1. ICA free Thursday night (under 18 is free every day).
  2. Walk the Harborwalk while the light’s good.
  3. Chinatown: My Thai Vegan Cafe + boba after.
<$25 for two

The kids · summer Friday

The drive-in double feature

  1. Mendon Twin Drive-In — two movies, one ticket.
  2. Pack your own snacks (vegetarian by default when you pack them).
  3. Radio on, windows down. That’s the whole plan.
$38 per carload

The dog + whoever

The perfect dog Saturday

  1. Early off-leash hour at Sheepfold (Middlesex Fells).
  2. Midday: Great Brook Farm loop — there’s an ice-cream stand.
  3. Wind down on a dog-welcome patio.
Free–$

Everyone · rainy Sunday

The rainy-day standard

  1. Harvard Art Museums — free for everybody.
  2. Diner lunch at Veggie Galaxy (vegan milkshakes settle arguments).
  3. A matinee at the Brattle.
~$10–20 each

How to use this

Read the tags & the timing

Every entry across the guides is labeled the same way so you can scan fast.

Price at a glance

Free $ · Cheap $$ · Moderate

The whole library skews free-to-moderate. Anything labeled $$ is a splurge-but-worth-it, not fine dining.

Timing, access & who it’s for

Best season Weekday tip Car-free ✓ 🐕 Dog OK 💕 Date-worthy

A Car-free ✓ tag means the T/bus/ferry genuinely works. 🐕 Dog OK means your dog is welcome (leash rules noted). 💕 flags the kids’ best bets.

The golden rule of doing this like a local: the same spot can be a joy on a Tuesday and a nightmare on a Saturday. Where crowds matter (beaches, trailheads, brunch, apple picking), the guides call out the weekday or early-morning move. When you can, go off-peak — you’re the one who lives here.
