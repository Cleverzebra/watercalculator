Timing & access

Best season Weekday tip Car-free ✓

A Car-free ✓ tag means you can realistically get there on the T, commuter rail, bus, or ferry.

The golden rule of doing this like a local: the same spot can be a joy on a Tuesday and a nightmare on a Saturday. Where crowds matter (beaches, popular trailheads, brunch, apple picking), we call out the weekday or early-morning move. When you can, go off-peak.

The year at a glance

🗓️ What’s on, month by month

A quick-scan calendar of the signature seasonal moves. Full details — dates, costs, and sources — live in the Events & Markets guide.

January

  • Free/cheap ice skating (Frog Pond, free DCR rinks)
  • Lunar New Year, Chinatown
  • Warm greenhouse escapes

February

  • Camellias at Lyman Estate greenhouses
  • Tower Hill orchid show opens
  • Maple sugaring begins

March

  • South Boston St. Patrick's Parade
  • Free DCR maple sugarin' days
  • Late-season skiing

April

  • Patriots' Day & Boston Marathon
  • Todd Farm flea season opens
  • Farmers markets return

May

  • Somerville Open Studios & PorchFest
  • Lilac Sunday, Arnold Arboretum
  • Brimfield #1; SoWa opens

June

  • Boston Pride
  • Cambridge River Festival
  • Beach season; North End feasts begin

July

  • Harborfest & Esplanade fireworks
  • Revere Sand Sculpting; Lowell Folk
  • Free outdoor movies & concerts

August

  • North End saint feasts
  • Caribbean Carnival; Moon Festival
  • County fairs begin

September

  • The Big E; Brimfield #3
  • Cambridge Carnival
  • Apple picking starts (go weekdays!)

October

  • HONK! & Head of the Charles
  • Topsfield Fair; foliage
  • Corn mazes & Halloween (skip Salem crowds)

November

  • Snowport & SoWa Winter Festival open
  • Holiday craft fairs ramp up
  • Winter farmers markets resume

December

  • Common Tree Lighting; Blink!
  • Holiday markets & light displays
  • First Night (Dec 31)
