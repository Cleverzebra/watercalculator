Guide 05 · Fairs, festivals, farmers & flea markets

Everything that’s on — all year.

State fairs, farmers markets, saint feasts, arts festivals, flea & craft markets, and the region's best thrifting — cross-checked against town, state, and organizer sources (not one calendar), with a month-by-month 2026 planner at the bottom.

Almost every festival & feast is a weekend event. The reliable weekday staples are the farmers markets (Copley Tue/Fri, Central Sq Mon, Charlestown Wed, Brookline Thu, Lexington Tue) and the daily indoor markets. The flea/vintage core is Sunday (Todd Farm, SoWa) plus Fri–Sat (Haymarket). Outdoor flea markets can cancel for weather — check the organizer the day before.

🎡 Massachusetts state & county fairs (2026)

Agricultural exhibits, 4-H, livestock, midways, demolition derbies, fair food, and craft/vendor halls. Admission is cheaper bought in advance online, and usually free for young kids.

🥬 Farmers markets

Nearly all Boston/GBFN markets accept SNAP/EBT with HIP matching plus WIC/Senior coupons. Winter (indoor) options exist year-round.

Boston & neighborhoods

  • Copley Square — Tue & Fri, 11–6, McKim Plaza (the biggest). ~May 15–Nov 24.
  • Boston Public Market — daily, indoor, year-round, 100 Hanover St.
  • Charlestown — Wed 2–6, Thompson Sq.
  • Jamaica Plain — Sat 12–3 & Tue 2–5, 677 Centre St.
  • Roslindale Village — Sat 9–1:30, Adams Park (Jun–Oct).
  • Dorchester (Ashmont, Codman Sq, Fields Corner) & Dudley/Nubian — varies.
  • Haymarket — Fri & Sat year-round, cash produce bazaar (cheap).

Cambridge / Somerville / suburbs

  • Central Square (Cambridge) — Mon 12–6, Bishop Allen Dr.
  • Harvard/Charles Sq — Thu 3–6.
  • Union Square (Somerville) — Sat 9–1, prepared food + music, EBT/HIP.
  • Lexington — Tue 2–6:30, Fletcher Park.
  • Brookline — Thu 1:30–6:30, Centre St West lot.
  • Newton — Sat 9:30–12:30, Newton North HS.
  • Arlington — Russell Commons (Arlington EATS program).
Winter markets: Somerville Winter Farmers Market (Saturdays, Nov–Apr, 65+ vendors indoors) and the daily Boston Public Market. Mass Farmers Markets keeps a full winter markets map.

Sources: Mass Farmers Markets · Boston.gov · Union Sq 2026

Antiques, makers & craft vendors

🪑 Flea, open-air & craft markets

📍 Rowley · Sundays, Apr–Nov

Todd Farm Antiques & Flea Market

Hundreds of dealers + year-round barn shops, 5am–2pm at 275 Main St. Parking $5 (free after 11am). Weather-dependent — check their page Fri/Sat.

Free to browse

thetoddfarm.com

📍 Brimfield · 3 weeks/yr

Brimfield Antique Flea Market

The big one — ~1 mile of dealers on Rte 20 (~75 min west). 2026: May 12–17 · Jul 14–19 · Sep 8–13. Fields open on staggered days; bring cash.

Some fields $5

brimfieldantiquefleamarket.com

📍 South End · Sundays, May–Nov

SoWa Open Market

Artists, makers, a farmers market, food trucks, and vintage — 11am–4/5pm at 500 Harrison Ave. The city's best maker/craft-vendor market.

Free

sowaboston.com

📍 South End · Sundays, year-round

SoWa Vintage Market

Boston's only weekly vintage/antiques market, 11am–5pm at 450 Harrison Ave (basement), 25–30 curated vendors.

Free

sowavintagemkt.com

📍 Union Sq, Somerville · daily

Bow Market

A permanent courtyard of ~30+ small independent makers & food, with rotating weekend maker pop-ups.

Free

bowmarketsomerville.com

📍 Seaport & South End · Nov–Dec

Holiday maker & craft markets

Snowport (Seaport, 125+ chalets), SoWa Winter Festival (100+ makers), Boston Women's Holiday Market (Faneuil Hall), plus church/school/library craft fairs across every suburb.

Free to browse

👖 Secondhand, thrift & vintage

The Garment District ★

📍 Cambridge (Kendall/MIT)

12,000+ sq ft, sorted by decade upstairs, costume shop down, and the legendary $2/lb "By the Pound" dig floor. The single best vintage destination for teens/young adults.

$🚆 Red Line

garmentdistrict.com

Nonprofit benefiting AIDS Action Committee — curated clothing, books, housewares, furniture.

$
