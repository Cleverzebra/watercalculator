🎃 October 2026

🔁 Every week in October

Core markets run all month (Lexington Tue till 6; Union Sq Sat thru Oct 31). Sundays: SoWa 🐕 (thru Nov 15), Todd Farm, Greenway's final month. ICA/Gardner Thursdays 💕, MFA Wednesdays, Coit 7:30.
